Privacy policy

ClamTotal is a self-hosted file-analysis service. It is designed so that nothing leaves your machine or the server.

What we collect

  • Files you upload — stored locally (by SHA-256) only as long as UPLOADS_TTL says: {{ ttl_phrase }}. A background job prunes them every minute.
  • Scan metadata — file name(s), hashes, verdicts, extracted indicators (URLs, domains, IPs, emails, registry keys) and static-analysis results, stored in the local SQLite database on the server.
