Privacy policy

ClamTotal is a self-hosted file-analysis service. It is designed so that nothing leaves your machine or your server.

What we collect

  • Files you upload — stored locally (by SHA-256) only as long as UPLOADS_TTL says: forever by default (-1), deleted right after scanning (0), or kept for N minutes. A background job prunes them every minute.
  • Scan metadata — file name(s), hashes, verdicts, extracted indicators (URLs, domains, IPs, emails, registry keys) and static-analysis results, stored in the local SQLite database on your server.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no login, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.
  • No data is sent to any third party. Scanning (ClamAV + local YARA rules) and all analysis run entirely on your own infrastructure — the app works fully offline.
  • Extracted indicators are defanged in the UI (hxxp://, [.]) so they are never fetched or clicked accidentally.
  • No samples or metadata are shared with anyone, including antivirus vendors.

Your controls

  • Set UPLOADS_TTL=0 to never keep uploaded files on disk.
  • Set SHOW_RECENT_SCANS=false to hide the recent-scans list.
  • Delete the database and upload directory at any time to erase all records.

Security note

Uploaded files may be malicious. ClamTotal is meant to run in an isolated environment (e.g. a container with no access to sensitive data), exactly like the real VirusTotal is used.
