Privacy policy

This policy describes what happens to your data when you use this website as a visitor or uploader.

What we collect from you

  • Files you upload — kept on the server only for the configured retention period (UPLOADS_TTL): forever by default, deleted immediately after scanning if set to 0, or kept for N minutes.
  • Scan results for those files — file name(s), hashes, detection verdicts, and extracted indicators (URLs, domains, IP addresses, email addresses, registry keys), stored in the site's local database.
  • Search queries you type into the search box are processed in memory only to answer the request and are not stored.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no login, no passwords, no profiles.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no telemetry.
  • No IP logging of your visits beyond what the server needs to answer the request; we do not build profiles of visitors.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere external. Scanning (ClamAV and local YARA rules) and all analysis run entirely on this server — no sample or metadata is sent to any third party or antivirus vendor.
  • Indicators shown in reports are defanged (hxxp://, [.]) so your browser never fetches them accidentally.

Your rights and controls

  • Uploaded files are deleted automatically after the configured retention period; simply do not upload files you want to keep private.
  • You may request deletion of any file's scan record and stored sample by its SHA-256.

Security note

Files uploaded to this service may be malicious. Do not upload files containing personal or confidential data unless you trust this instance.
